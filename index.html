♟ Chess Rules Guide

Objective

Checkmate your opponent's king or force them into a draw.

Piece Movement

  • ♔ King: One square in any direction
  • ♕ Queen: Any number of squares in any direction
  • ♖ Rook: Horizontally or vertically
  • ♗ Bishop: Diagonally
  • ♘ Knight: L-shape (2+1), can jump over pieces
  • ♙ Pawn: Forward 1 (or 2 on first move), captures diagonally

Special Rules

  • Check: King is under attack
  • Checkmate: King in check with no escape
  • Stalemate: No legal moves but not in check (draw)
  • Castling: Not yet implemented
  • En Passant: Not yet implemented
  • Promotion: Not yet implemented
